This is Cheryl. I am currently a PhD Candidate in Communication at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://commarts.wisc.edu/staff/shea-cheryl).

I care about **civic engagement, resistance, and resilience** through the lens of media and information structure. 
In the world, we all have to rely on others to get knowledge; that knowledge constructs our ways of seeing, our belonging, our beliefs, and hence our care and behaviors. 
Specifically, I look into how our way of seeking understanding and beliefs, together with our way of hunting information via different media and communication channels, supports civic society — whether it is thriving or challenged by diminishing political opportunities.

Hence, you can see me researching the role of bookstores when political opportunities diminish, 
online platforms and physical sites that foster civic engagement, or digital platforms enabling democratic outreach. 
In the end, we all rely on communication and knowledge to slowly build our society, 
and I'm interested in seeing what societies we can build collectively, for the good of others.

My work has been presented at international conferences, including ICA and AEJMC, and has won the Top Paper Award from the *International Communication Association*.
You can also see my research published in peer-reviewed journals such as *Digital Journalism*, *Information, Communication & Society*, *British Food Journal* and *Chinese Journal of Communication* etc.
